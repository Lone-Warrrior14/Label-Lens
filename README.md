# 🥗 LabelLens — AI-Powered Food Label Intelligence

**Look beyond the label. Understand what you eat.**

LabelLens is an AI-powered food label analyser designed to help consumers make informed decisions about packaged foods. By analysing ingredient lists, nutritional information, and declared allergens, LabelLens transforms complicated food labels into understandable insights, transparent ratings, and product comparisons.

Built with Indian consumers in mind, LabelLens interprets ingredient order according to applicable food-labelling rules, highlights potential concerns, and helps users look beyond marketing claims.

## 🎯 The Problem

Food packaging often contains complex ingredient names, nutritional tables, unfamiliar additives, and marketing claims that can be difficult for everyday consumers to interpret.

Consumers may struggle to understand:

- Which ingredients appear first in a product's composition.
- Whether a product contains high levels of sugar, sodium, or saturated fat.
- What unfamiliar ingredients and food additives are used for.
- Which allergens are declared on the packaging.
- How two similar products differ nutritionally.
- Whether a product's marketing claims tell the whole story.

## 💡 Our Solution

LabelLens combines AI-powered label interpretation with structured nutritional analysis to make food information easier to understand.

Users can upload or photograph a product label and receive an analysis that explains its ingredients, identifies relevant nutritional concerns, and presents the evidence behind its ratings.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📸 AI-Powered Label Scanning
- Analyse photos of packaged-food labels.
- Extract ingredient lists and nutrition facts using OCR or vision models.
- Convert unstructured label information into structured data.
- Highlight unclear or unreadable information instead of silently guessing.

### 🔬 Ingredient Intelligence
- Explain unfamiliar ingredients in plain language.
- Describe the typical function of listed additives where reliable information is available.
- Interpret ingredient order according to applicable labelling requirements.
- Highlight ingredients that deserve further attention without automatically treating unfamiliar ingredients as harmful.

### 📊 Nutritional Analysis
- Analyse available values for sugar, sodium, saturated fat, protein, fibre, and other nutrients.
- Compare nutritional values per 100 g or 100 ml and per serving where the label provides sufficient information.
- Highlight potentially concerning values using transparent criteria.
- Explain the difference between nutritional facts and broader health claims.

### ⚠️ Potential Concern Detection
- Highlight declared allergens.
- Identify relevant nutritional concerns.
- Flag missing, ambiguous, or unreadable label information.
- Explain why each issue was raised and which label evidence supports it.
